# Reporting a weakness

Some of our collection checks work by reading what a client's AI reports and
refusing answers that read as shortcuts. The exact wording they match on is
not published, because publishing it would publish the way around it.

If you find a way to defeat a check, or any weakness in the method that
someone could exploit, please do not open a public issue. Write to
cpa@chiarohq.com and we will respond quickly. Once it is fixed, we are glad
to credit you in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).

Everything else, including hard criticism of the method, belongs in the
open: see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
